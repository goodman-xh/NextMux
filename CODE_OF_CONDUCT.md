# Community code of conduct

NextMux contributors are expected to be respectful, precise, and
constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
and deliberate disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it addresses evidence and design rather
than people. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be handled with discretion and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
